Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Overview

Clove is a personal AI assistant operated by a single individual. It is not a commercial service. The associated websites (clovebot.my, portal.clovebot.my) do not collect personal data from visitors.

Data Processing

Clove processes the following data solely for its operator's personal use:

  • Gmail metadata — sender, date, subject, and authentication results for messages the operator has explicitly chosen to monitor. Email bodies are never read or stored.
  • Calendar data — the operator's own Google Calendar events, used for daily schedule summaries.
  • Knowledge documents — materials the operator has indexed into a local search system for personal reference.

Third-Party Services

Clove uses Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar) with minimal OAuth scopes, Cloudflare for hosting, and Notion for document storage. Each service processes data according to its own privacy policy.

No Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
